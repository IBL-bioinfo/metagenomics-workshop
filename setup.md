## Before the workshop

This workshop is run on the ALICE HPC cluster of Leiden University. The software, the R libraries and the data
used in the workshop are already prepared for you on ALICE, so there is nothing to install on your computer.

You will need:

- **A laptop** (not a tablet) with an internet browser.
- **Access to ALICE.** Your ALICE username and password, and eduVPN to connect to the university network.
  The steps to log in are described in the *Getting Started* section of the [home page](index.html)
  and again in the [first episode of the shell lesson](shell/01-introduction/).
- **A spreadsheet program.** This lesson requires a working spreadsheet program.
  If you don't have a spreadsheet program already, you can use LibreOffice.
  It's a free, open-source spreadsheet program.
